## About

NSE Market Hub brings live Nairobi Securities Exchange data into a single mobile-friendly dashboard — designed for Kenyan investors who want real-time prices, AI-backed insight, and alerts delivered straight to Discord, without needing to track the market manually.

## Coverage

🇰🇪 **NSE Kenya** — 12 listed companies, including:

- Safaricom
- Equity Group
- KCB Group
- EABL (East African Breweries)
- BAT Kenya (British American Tobacco)
- *...and 7 more*

## Key Features

- 📊 **Live Market Data** — real-time price feeds for covered NSE-listed companies.
- 🤖 **AI-Driven Analysis** — automated insights and commentary on price movement and trends.
- 🔔 **Discord Alerts** — instant notifications pushed to Discord when prices move.
- 💼 **Portfolio Tracker** — track your holdings and performance across covered stocks.
- 📱 **Mobile-First Dashboard** — built for quick checks on the go.
